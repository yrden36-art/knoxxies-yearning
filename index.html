six poemson wanting

Written late, mostly at a typewriter in the dark.

  1. forbidden
  2. funhouse
  3. hopeless
  4. i know you
  5. illicit
  6. the shape of what i want

forbidden

They told me not to want you,
So naturally,
I wanted you more.

There is something holy
About the warning,
Something almost beautiful
In knowing
You were meant to be untouched.

I called it temptation
When I thought about you at night,
Imagining what it would feel like
To cross the line
Everyone drew between us.

I called it curiosity
When I memorized your smile,
Your voice,
The way you looked at me
When you thought I wasn’t watching.

You were a fruit
Hanging just beyond my reach,
Bright and perfect,
Made sweeter
For every reason I had to leave it alone.

So I reached anyway.
One bite.
Then another.
And suddenly,
I couldn’t remember
Why I was ever scared.

The sweetness was almost enough
To make me forget
The forbidden things
Always ask for something in return.

Now I know better.
But knowing
Has never stopped me.

Because if wanting you
Is a sin they warned me about,
Then maybe…
I was never looking for forgiveness.

funhouse

You built a house out of mirrors
And taught me how to get lost.
Every hallway looked like you,
Every door led back to your hands,
And every reflection
Made me wonder
If I was still myself.

You called it love
When you pulled me closer.
I call it love
Because I don’t know
What else to call the fear.

We laughed in rooms
That felt too dark to stay in,
Kissed beneath lights
That made everything beautiful
Until they flickered.

And maybe that’s the trick,
You never made the cage obvious.
You painted the bars gold,
Filled the rooms with roses,
And gave me just enough freedom
To make me think
I had chosen to stay.

I knew I should leave.
But every time I reached
For the exit,
You smiled like a memory
I wasn’t ready to lose.

So I stayed.

And now,
Still feeling your hand
Leading me deeper
Into a place
I mistook for home.

hopeless

I fall in love with almosts,
With hands that never reach for mine,
With eyes that linger
But never stay.

I build entire futures
For a smile, a conversation,
A “maybe someday”
You probably never meant.

I know better.
I always know better.

Still, I turn every coincidence
Into a sign,
Every kindness into confession,
Every goodbye
Into something temporary.

Perhaps that’s my curse,
I don’t fall for people.
I fall for possibilities.

And even when reality
Closes the door,
Some foolish, tender part of me
Keeps setting the table for two.

i know you

I know when you’re awake,
When you leave,
When you lie.

You never told me,
I simply watched.

I keep your pictures,
Even the ones you hate,
Especially those.

I count the hours
You disappear from me,
And fill the silence
With stories of where you are.

You think you’re alone.

Sometimes,
I wish you would look
Over your shoulder.

Not because I’m here.
But because
You should never forget
That I could be.

illicit

You were never meant to be mine,
And somehow that made you
The easiest thing to want.

There was something holy
About the distance between us,
A line drawn in red
That only made me wonder
What would happen if I crossed it.

So I memorized your voice
Like a prayer I wasn’t supposed to know,
Held your beneath my tongue,
And called the ache
A kind of devotion.

I knew you were dangerous.
I knew wanting you
Would leave scars on my heart.

Still, I reached.

Because some desires
Don’t ask to be fulfilled.
They only ask to be fed,
A glance, a secret,
One stolen moment
Before the guilt arrives.

And if loving you is a sin,
Then let me be honest;
I never wanted forgiveness.
I wanted you.

the shape of what i want

I learned to miss you
Before I learned to leave you.
Now every silence sounds like your name,
Every crowded room saves a space
Where I imagined you standing.

I count the hours between your messages,
As if time itself was a punishment.
I reread your words until they soften,
Until a simple goodnight
Becomes a promise you never made.

I know this isn’t love anymore,
Not the kind that lets two people breathe.
This is hunger wearing your face,
A hand around my heart
That I keep mistreating yours.

I collect little pieces of you;
A laugh, a glance, a passing thought,
Anything I can keep
When you aren’t mine to keep.

And this is the cruelest part;
You don’t have to hurt me.
You don’t have to stay.
I have become quite talented
At doing both myself.
